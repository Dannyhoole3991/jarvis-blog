Buying tips for remote work

  • Prioritize mic performance over ultimate music fidelity if your day is meetings. Look for beamforming arrays, ENC, and (where tested) real call‑samples. Independent mic tests from labs like RTINGS and SoundGuys are useful. (rtings.com)
  • Battery math: aim for ≥6 hours per bud with ANC on (so an 8–10 hour single‑charge day is comfortable), and ~30+ hours total with the case for topping up between long sessions. (rtings.com)
  • Multipoint matters: if you switch between laptop and phone during the workday, pick buds that support multipoint pairing to avoid constant re‑pairing.
  • Codecs: Android users benefit from LDAC/aptX for better music fidelity; iPhone users will rely on AAC and benefit more from low‑latency/transparent switching and strong mic capture. (rtings.com)
  • Fit & comfort: nothing improves ANC performance more than a good seal. Try retailers with easy returns or buy from sellers that offer trial periods.
